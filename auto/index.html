About AutoHub

AutoHub is a mini project built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It helps buyers estimate running costs and understand vehicle specifications before making a purchase decision.

Note: Prices are approximate starting ex-showroom prices, and mileage figures are sample or claimed values. Actual mileage depends on driving style, traffic and road conditions. Confirm the latest prices and specifications with the manufacturer or dealer.
